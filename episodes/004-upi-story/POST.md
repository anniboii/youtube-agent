## Facts checked
- NPCI's switch routes UPI payments; PIN is encrypted by NPCI's common-library keypad (UPI CL spec).
- Interbank settlement is netted and settled via RBI in batches; 10 cycles/day from 3 Nov 2025 (NPCI OC 222, via Business Today).
- ₹500/₹480 crore is a labelled example ("maan lo").
